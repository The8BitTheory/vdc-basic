VDC-BASIC Converter

Turns a picture into a file that a VDC-BASIC program can load. Everything happens in this page; no data leaves your computer.

1. Picture

PNG, BMP or GIF in its final size. Colours are matched to the 16 VDC colours. The picture is not scaled.

2. What should it become?

Pixels of this colour, and pixels that are transparent in the file, are left out.

The sheet is read cell by cell, left to right, top to bottom. The first cell is the space (code 32).

3. Result

No picture loaded.
