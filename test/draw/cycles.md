# VDM/VDL/VDR/VDB: cycles in VICE (preliminary)

Generated by `test/draw/run_draw.py --cycles F`. Stopwatch of the VICE monitor from `draw_begin` (after parsing
and checking) to `draw_end`, IRQ disabled for the measurement (I flag via the monitor), minimum of
8 runs per case. 1 MHz, driver interpreted. The values are **CPU cycles**.

**Preliminary:** VICE models VDC access in a simplified way (busy time per reg 31 access in the visible
area, 43 clocks instead of 4 in the border); values on real hardware are pending. Targets from docs/v3/P2c-draw.md,
section 7; deviations are explained there in section 12.
